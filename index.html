How Step 1 works

  1. All 52 cards (no jokers) are shuffled and stacked face-down in the middle.
  2. A random player starts. Going clockwise, each player picks the top card and puts it on top of their own stack.
  3. Only the top card of each stack is public — to everyone, at all times. Everything under it belongs to its owner alone.
  4. After every pick an exchange window opens: you may give your top card to a player whose top card is exactly one rank lower. Suit is ignored. It is a permanent transfer landing on top of their stack — so chains happen.
  5. Giving is voluntary and unlimited. The window closes only when every player holding cards has passed. Then the next player picks.
  6. Play continues until the middle pile is empty. Everyone keeps their stack → Step 2: Donkeyyy.
